Dear Baby,

Happy Monthsary to us, baby! 🥹 Honestly, I don’t think these letters and words I say can fully express my love for you. And I’m here, writing this letter for you, because you need to know how deeply grateful I am to have you in my life.

🌷

Every time we spend time together, whether we’re having deep talks, laughs, or enjoying each other’s presence, I really do appreciate these moments with you.

You have become so important and such a beautiful part of my life. You are someone I look forward to talking to, someone who can make me smile without trying, and your love makes everything lighter.

These times made me realize how much you have become a part of my life, and I’m so grateful I can call you my baby.

♡

I want you to know how much I appreciate you—not only for the things you do for me, but for who you are.

I admire the way you show your true self to me, the way you care so much for the people you love, and all those things that express you.

You never have to be perfect for me. You never have to change yourself just to feel worthy of being loved. I want you to know that you’re already enough.

I appreciate and love you for who you are, including those parts you’re struggling to appreciate. You deserve to be loved, understood, and treated with respect.

🌸

I also know our relationship is not always perfect. There are times when we misunderstand each other, or struggle to express what we’re feeling.

But I always hope when that happens, we choose to understand rather than not talk to each other and leave things unfixed, and fix it together rather than immediately giving up on each other.

I don’t promise that I will always know exactly what to say or that I will never make mistakes. I’m still learning, becoming a better version of myself.

But I can promise that I will continue trying.

♡

I want to know how to love you better, become someone who makes you feel safe, and appreciated.

If you ever have a difficult day, I want you to know that you can talk to me. If something makes you happy, I want to hear about it.

If you accomplish something, I want to celebrate it with you. If you feel like giving up, I want to remind you of how strong and capable you are.

I want to be someone who supports you not only when everything is going well, but also when things become difficult.

🌷

Thank you for every time you make me laugh, smile, cheer me up, lighten my day, every moment of reassurance, and every moment we created together.

Thank you for giving me your time and efforts and letting me in your life. I genuinely appreciate every moment you choose to spend with me.

Sometimes, I wish you could see yourself the way I see you. Maybe then you would understand why I admire and appreciate you so much.

♡

You are someone I can rely on every time I’m having my hardest days.

I don’t want to rush through the months just so we can say we’ve been together for a certain amount of time. I want to appreciate each month as it comes.

I want to experience more moments with you, learn more about you, grow with you, and continue loving and creating memories that we can look back on someday.

♡
